# Orbitix Raw: one-knob stereo width & air

![Orbitix Raw free one-knob stereo width & air UI](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RemiBlaze/OrbitixRaw/main/orbitixraw-ui-screenshot.png)

**One knob for instant stereo width and air, mono-safe and level-matched.**

Orbitix Raw is the free, one-knob version of Orbitix, Remi Blaze's spatial and air processor. Turn a single **SPACE** knob and your mix opens up: wider, airier, and more three-dimensional, without a volume jump.

**macOS** (Apple Silicon and Intel): AU, VST3, CLAP, AAX, Standalone. Signed and notarized by Apple.

**Windows** 10 and 11, 64-bit: VST3, CLAP, Standalone. Authenticode signed.

AAX ships on macOS only.

---

## 🚀 Download & Install

Go to the [latest release](https://github.com/RemiBlaze/OrbitixRaw/releases/latest) and pick your platform.

**macOS**
1. Download **`OrbitixRaw_Installer.pkg`**.
2. Double-click it and follow the installer. It is signed and notarized by Apple, so it installs cleanly with no security warnings.
3. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins. Orbitix Raw appears under **Remi Blaze**.

**Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit**
1. Download **`OrbitixRaw_Installer.exe`**.
2. Run it and follow the installer. It is Authenticode signed.
3. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins. Orbitix Raw appears under **Remi Blaze**.

No dongle and no extra account on either platform.

Full guide: **[remiblaze.com/support](https://remiblaze.com/support/)**.

---

## 🎛️ What It Does
One big **SPACE** knob. As you turn it up, Orbitix Raw spreads the stereo image and adds high-frequency shimmer together, so the sound gets wider and airier at the same time. It runs in a mono-safe, level-stable mode and applies a gentle output bias, so it just sounds bigger, never louder, and stays mono-compatible. Works on synths, vocals, pads, drums, and the full mix.

The SPACE knob is the only control you need; everything else is tuned internally.
